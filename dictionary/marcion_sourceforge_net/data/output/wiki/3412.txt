¶ ⟨ⳉ⟩, 27th letter of alphabet, used in ⟪A⟫ only⟦;⟧ no name recorded. Derived from ⟦hierogl⟧{annotation: hieroglyphic} ⟨ẖ⟩ & ⟨ḫ⟩, as ⟨ⳉⲏⲃⲥ⟩, ⟨ⳉⲏⲙ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲓⳉⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲩⳉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲥⳉⲉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲡⲱⲥⳉ⟩, ⟨ⲱⲛⳉ⟩.

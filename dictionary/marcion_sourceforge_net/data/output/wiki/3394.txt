¶ ⟨ⲓ⟩ or ⟨ⲉⲓ⟩, ninth letter of alphabet, called ⟨ⲓⲱⲧⲁ⟩ (⟦Leyd 434⟧{reference: Leyd}, ⟦Tri 483⟧{reference: Tri}, ⟦My 66⟧{reference: My}), ⟨ⲓⲟⲧⲁ⟩ (⟦Stern p. 418⟧{reference: Stern}), ⟨ⲓⲁⲩⲇⲁ⟩ (⟦K 1⟧{reference: K}), ⟨يوطة⟩, (⟦P 44 129⟧{reference: P}, ⟦Tri ‹l c›⟧{reference: Tri}), ⟨يوضة⟩ (⟦Stern ‹l c›⟧{reference: Stern}). As numeral ⟦m⟧{annotation: masculine} & ⟦f⟧{annotation: feminine} = ⟨gloss: ten⟩ ⟪B⟫⟪F⟫, rarely ⟪S⟫ except in semi-⟦Gk⟧{annotation: Greek} documents. On relation to ⟦hierogl⟧{annotation: hieroglyphic} half-consonants & vowels ⟦‹v›⟧{annotation: vide} ⟦Sethe ‹Verbum› 1 90–108⟧{reference: Sethe Verbum}. In ⟪S⟫ (mostly archaic) ⟪A⟫⟪F⟫ ⟨ⲉⲓ⟩ after vowel often for ⟨ⲓ⟩, as ⟨ⲁⲉⲓ-⟩, ⟨ⲉⲣⲟⲉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲏⲉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲕⲟⲩⲉⲓ⟩⟦;⟧ similarly ⟨ⲉⲉⲓ-⟩ for ⟨ⲉⲓ⟩⟦;⟧ converse (⟪S⟫ archaic) as ⟨ⲣⲟⲓ̈ⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲁⲓ̈ⲛ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲩⲓ̈.⟩.

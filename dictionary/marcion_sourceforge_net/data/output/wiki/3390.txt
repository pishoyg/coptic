¶ ⟨ⲉ⟩, fifth letter of alphabet, called ⟨ⲉⲓ⟩ ⟪S⟫ (⟦My 62, 63⟧{reference: My}), ⟨ⲉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲉⲓⲉ⟩ ⟪B⟫ (⟦K 1⟧{reference: K}, ⟦Stern p 418⟧{reference: Stern}), ⟨اية⟩, ⟨ايية⟩ (⟦P 43 238, 44 133⟧{reference: P}). As numeral ⟦m⟧{annotation: masculine} & ⟦f⟧{annotation: feminine} = ⟨gloss: five⟩ ⟪B⟫⟪F⟫, rarely ⟪S⟫ (⟦Br 130⟧{reference: Br}). Represents vowels unwritten in earlier ⟦hierogl⟧{annotation: hieroglyphic}, though visible in certain half-consonants in later periods. Dialectal equivalents:
¶ ⟨bullet: a.⟩ ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲉ⟩ = ⟪A⟫ ⟨ⲁ⟩ rare, as ⟨ⲉ-⟩ ⟦prep⟧{annotation: preposition}, ⟨ⲉⲛⲉϩ⟩⟦;⟧ converse often ⟪A⟫, as ⟨ⲁϩⲟⲙ⟩, ⟨ⲕⲁⲕⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲁϥ⟩, ⟨ⲧⲥⲁⲃⲟ⟩⟦;⟧ ⟪F⟫, as ⟨ⲃⲁⲗ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲁⲁϫⲉ⟩, ⟨ϩⲁⲗⲁⲧⲉ⟩ & in ⟦Gk⟧{annotation: Greek} as ⟨ⲗⲓⲧⲣⲉ⟩ (⟦BM 530⟧{reference: BM}), ⟨ⲡⲁⲣⲉ-⟩ (⟦‹ib› 610⟧{reference: BM}), ⟨ⲥⲁⲣⲉ⟩ (Sarah ⟦‹ib› 580⟧{reference: BM}).
¶ ⟨bullet: b.⟩ ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲉ⟩ = ⟪F⟫ ⟨ⲏ⟩, as ⟨ⲁⲡⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲃⲗϫⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲉⲉⲡⲉ⟩, ⟨ϣⲉ⟩.
¶ ⟨bullet: c.⟩ ⟪S⟫ ⟨-ⲉ⟩ final = ⟪B⟫⟪F⟫ ⟨-ⲓ⟩, as ⟨ⲙⲉⲉⲩⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲣⲱⲙⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲥϩⲓⲙⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲁⲕⲁⲣⲉ⟩ (⟨Μακάριος⟩), ⟨ϫⲓⲥⲉ⟩⟦;⟧ or = ⟪B⟫ ⟨ⲉⲓ⟩, as ⟨ⲣⲡⲉ⟩, ⟨ϩⲉ⟩ ⟨gloss: fall⟩.
¶ In ⟦Gk⟧{annotation: Greek} words:
¶ ⟨bullet: d.⟩ ⟨ⲉ⟩ for ⟨ⲁ⟩ rarely, as ⟨ⲉⲛⲉⲅⲉ⟩ (⟦Wor 178⟧{reference: Wor}, ⟦J pass⟧{reference: J})⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲁⲣⲅⲁⲧⲏⲥ⟩ (⟦Kr 245⟧{reference: Kr}).
¶ ⟨bullet: e.⟩ for ⟨ⲁⲓ⟩ (oftenest, ⟦esp⟧{annotation: especially} ⟪B⟫), as ⟨ⲁⲣⲭⲉⲟⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲅⲉⲛⲏ⟩ (⟨καινή⟩), ⟨ⲉⲑⲏⲣ⟩, ⟨ⲭⲉⲣⲉ⟩, or in passive ⟦vb⟧{annotation: verb}, as ⟨ⲁⲥⲡⲁⲍⲉⲥⲑⲉ⟩⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲁⲓⲡⲉⲛⲟⲥ⟩ (⟨ἔπαι.⟩), ⟨ⲡⲁⲓⲇⲉⲥ⟩ (⟨πέδας⟩).
¶ ⟨bullet: f.⟩ for ⟨ⲏ⟩, as ⟨ⲕⲟⲙⲉⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲕⲉⲩⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲧⲉⲙⲉⲩⲉ⟩ (⟨δημ.⟩ ⟦BMis 219⟧{reference: B Mis}), ⟨ϩⲩⲡⲉⲣⲉⲧⲏⲥ⟩⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲁⲓⲣⲏⲥⲓⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲑⲏⲥⲡⲉⲥⲓⲟⲥ⟩ (⟦LDi 275⟧{reference: L Di}).
¶ ⟨bullet: g.⟩ for ⟨ⲉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲓ⟩, as ⟨ⲁⲡⲟⲇⲉⲝⲓⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲅⲉⲧⲛⲓⲁ⟩, ⟨ⲉⲛⲱⲭⲗⲉ⟩⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲁⲛⲇⲓⲗⲓⲅⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲓⲡⲓⲥⲧⲁⲣⲙⲁ⟩ (⟨ἐπίσταλμα⟩ ⟦PLond 4 481⟧{reference: P Lond}).
¶ ⟨bullet: h.⟩ for ⟨ⲩ⟩, as ⟨ⲉⲥⲉⲭⲁⲍⲉ⟩ (⟨ἡσυχ.⟩), ⟨ⲕⲉⲕⲗⲟⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲉⲕⲉ⟩ (⟨ζυγή⟩ ⟦J 16 23⟧{reference: J})⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲇⲣⲁⲡⲩⲍⲁ⟩, ⟨ⲩⲣⲏⲙⲟⲥ⟩.
¶ ⟨bullet: i.⟩ for ⟨ⲉⲩ⟩, as ⟨ⲗⲉⲕⲟⲫⲟⲣⲉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲕⲉⲟⲥ⟩.
¶ ⟨bullet: j.⟩ for ⟨ⲟⲓ⟩, as ⟨ⲅⲉⲛⲉⲧⲟ ⲉⲡⲉⲅⲉ⟩ (⟨ἐποίκιον⟩ ⟦J 81 3⟧{reference: J}), ⟨ⲙⲉⲛⲧⲉⲅⲉ⟩ (⟨μέντοιγε⟩ ⟦Kr 157⟧{reference: Kr}), ⟨ⲥⲧⲉⲭⲉ⟩.
¶ Prosthetically, before ⟨ⲙ⟩, ⟨ⲛ⟩, ⟨ⲗ⟩, ⟨ⲣ⟩, sometimes ⟨ⲡ⟩, ⟨ⲧ⟩ (⟦Pcod xi⟧{reference: P cod}), ⟨ⲃ⟩ (⟪S⟫ ⟦?⟧{annotation: perhaps, possibly} archaic), ⟨ⲉ⟩ is represented by line over following consonant, as ⟨ⲙ̄ⲙⲁⲩ⟩, ⟨ⲙ̄ⲧⲟⲛ⟩, ⟨ⲛ̄ϭⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲗ̄ⲗⲏⲃ⟩, ⟨ⲣ̄ⲣⲟ⟩, ⟨ⲃ̄ⲃⲁⲗ⟩⟦;⟧ ⟦‹cf›⟧{annotation: confer} ⟦sim⟧{annotation: similar in use or in meaning to the last quoted instance} ⟨ⲛ̄ⲕⲁⲕⲓⲛ⟩, ⟨ⲕ̄ⲕⲗⲏⲥⲓⲁ⟩, ⟨ⲙ̄ⲡⲟⲣⲓⲁ⟩, ⟨ⲣ̄ⲅⲁⲥⲓⲁ⟩⟦;⟧ elsewhere before 2 consonants, mostly ⟪B⟫, as ⟨ⲉϣⲣⲱ⟩ (⟦Ez 36 17⟧{bible: Ezekiel 36:17}), ⟨ⲉϩⲕⲟ⟩ (⟦C 86 238⟧{reference: C}), ⟨ⲉϩⲗⲓ⟩ (⟦Mus 34 208⟧{reference: Mus}), ⟨ⲉϩⲣⲟϣ⟩ (⟦TstAb 246⟧{reference: Tst Ab})⟦;⟧ ⟪S⟫ as ⟨ⲉⲡⲣⲱ⟩ (⟦Ryl 94 101⟧{reference: Ryl}), ⟨ⲉϣⲧⲉⲕⲟ⟩ (⟦Miss 4 619⟧{reference: Miss}), ⟨ⲉϩⲓⲉⲓⲃ⟩ (⟦1 Kg 7 9⟧{bible: First Samuel 7:9}), ⟨ⲉϩⲓⲱⲱ⸗⟩ (⟦Mor 20 42⟧{reference: Mor}, ⟦‹ib› 54 56⟧{reference: Mor} ⟦&c⟧{annotation: et cetera})⟦;⟧ ⟦sim⟧{annotation: similar in use or in meaning to the last quoted instance} ⟨ⲉⲃⲗⲁⲡⲧⲓⲛ⟩ (⟦Va 57 12⟧{reference: Va}, ⟦‹cf›⟧{annotation: confer} ⟦C 89 13 ‹inf›⟧{reference: C}), ⟨ⲉⲃⲣⲁⲃⲓⲟⲛ⟩ (⟦MG 25 319⟧{reference: MG}), ⟨ⲉϩⲣⲉⲇⲓⲕⲟⲥ⟩ (⟨αἱρετ.⟩ ⟦EW 173⟧{reference: EW}). Also represented by superline if toneless between consonants ⟪S⟫, as ⟨ⲥⲱⲣⲙ̄⟩, but ⟪B⟫ ⟨ⲥⲱⲣⲉⲙ⟩⟦;⟧ in good ⟪S⟫⟪B⟫ ⟦MSS⟧{annotation: manuscripts} also before suffix, ⟨ⲥⲟⲣⲙⲕ̄⟩. Wrongly between consonants, as ⟨ⲏⲣⲉⲡ⟩ (often ⟨ⲏⲣⲏⲡ⟩), ⟨ⲡⲉⲥⲁⲧⲉ⟩ (⟨ⲯⲁⲧⲉ⟩), ⟨ⲥⲁⲣⲉⲝ⟩ (⟦KKS 183⟧{reference: KKS}), ⟨ⲥⲉⲗⲉⲡⲓⲛ⟩ (⟨σπλήν⟩), ⟨ⲭⲉⲣⲱⲛⲱⲥ⟩ (⟦?⟧{annotation: perhaps, possibly} ⟨χρόνος⟩ ⟦PLond 4 524⟧{reference: P Lond}), ⟨ϣⲉⲣⲉⲡ⟩ (⟦Kr 7⟧{reference: Kr}), ⟨ϩⲉⲛⲉⲟⲩ⟩ (⟦‹ib› 225⟧{reference: Kr}). In late, ⟦esp⟧{annotation: especially} ⟪F⟫ texts ⟨ⲉ-⟩ often for ⟨ⲛ̄-⟩, as ⟨ⲡⲣⲁⲛ ⲉⲡⲛ︤ⲉ︥⟩, ⟨ⲙⲉⲛⲉⲥⲛⲉⲩ⟩, ⟨ϩⲉⲡⲉⲃⲏⲓ⟩⟦;⟧ rare in early: ⟦PS 55⟧{reference: PS} ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲉⲡⲛ︤ⲉ︥⟩, 200 ⟨ⲉⲫⲁⲉ⟩.

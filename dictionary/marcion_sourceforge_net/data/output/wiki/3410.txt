¶ ⟨ϣ⟩, 25th letter of alphabet, called ⟨ϣⲁⲓ⟩, ⟨ϣⲉⲓ⟩ (⟦K 1⟧{reference: K}, ⟦Stern p 418⟧{reference: Stern}) ⟪B⟫, ⟨شاي⟩ (⟦Stern ‹l c›⟧{reference: Stern}).
¶ Derived from ⟦hierogl⟧{annotation: hieroglyphic} ⟨š⟩, as ⟨ϣⲱⲙ⟩, ⟨ϣⲟⲟⲩⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲣⲁϣⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲩⲱϣⲧ⟩, ⟨ⲧⲱϣ⟩⟦;⟧ oftener from ⟨ḫ⟩, as ⟨ϣⲓ⟩, ⟨ϣⲱⲗⲙ⟩, ⟨ⲛϣⲟⲧ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲱϣⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲡⲱⲣϣ⟩ (original sound still in ⟪A⟫, as ⟨ⳉⲁⲙⲧ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲁⲁⲛⳉ⟩, ⟨ⲱⲃⳉ⟩ & in names, as ⟨Παμχῆμις⟩, ⟨Χεμτσνεῦς⟩).
¶ Varies with initial ⟨ⲥ⟩ (⟦‹q v›⟧{annotation: quod vide}), as ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲥⲱϣ⟩ ⟪B⟫ ⟨ϣⲱϣ⟩, ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲥⲁϣϥ⟩ ⟪B⟫ ⟨ϣⲁϣϥ⟩, ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲥϭⲏⲣ⟩ ⟪B⟫ ⟨ϣϭⲏⲣ⟩, ⟪A⟫ ⟨ⲥⲃⲉ⟩ ⟪S⟫⟪B⟫ ⟨ϣϥⲉ⟩, ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲥⲁϣ⟩ ⟪F⟫ ⟨ϣⲉϣ⟩, ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲥⲱϣⲉ⟩ ⟪F⟫ ⟨ϣⲱϣⲓ⟩⟦;⟧ rarely in other positions, as ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲱⲥϭ⟩ ⟪B⟫ ⟨ⲱϣϫ⟩.
¶ Replaces ⟨ⲥ⟩ (abnormal), as ⟪A⟫ ⟨ⲛⲧϣ⟩, ⟨ϩⲁϣⲉ⟩ (⟦RChamp 539⟧{reference: R Champ}).
¶ For ⟨-ϣⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲛϣ⟩ (⟦Mor 17 74⟧{reference: Mor}, ⟦var⟧{annotation: variant, in same dialect} ⟨ⲟⲛϣⲥ⟩ ⟦P 129^(14) 92⟧{reference: P}), ⟨ⲟϣ⟩ (⟦J 3 60, 68 97⟧{reference: J})⟦;⟧ added to ⟨ⲥ⟩, as ⟨ⲥϣϭⲣⲁϩⲧ⟩ (⟦Mor 31 61⟧{reference: Mor}), ⟨ϣⲥϭⲏⲣ⟩ (⟦Aeg 261⟧{reference: Aeg}), ⟨ⲥϣϭⲏⲣ⟩ (⟦Mor 41 25⟧{reference: Mor}). Doubled as initial ⟪B⟫: ⟨ϣϣⲏⲛ⟩, ⟨ϣϣⲱⲟⲩ⟩, ⟪F⟫ ⟨ϣϣⲉⲛ⟩ (⟦Eccl 2 5⟧{bible: Ecclesiastes 2:5}).
¶ Replaces ⟨ⲭ⟩, as ⟨ⲁⲣϣⲏⲙⲁⲛⲇⲣⲓⲧⲏⲥ⟩, ⟨ϣⲏⲣⲁ⟩ (gloss on ⟨ⲭⲏⲣⲁ⟩ ⟦Stern 16⟧{reference: Stern}).
¶ Replaces ⟨ϩ⟩ (rare), as ⟨ⲁϣⲓ⟩ (⟨ⲁϩⲉ⟩ ⟦Pcod Mor 5⟧{reference: Pcod Mor}), ⟨ϣⲱⲃⲥ⟩ (⟦Eccl 6 4⟧{bible: Ecclesiastes 6:4} ⟪F⟫), ⟨ⲉⲧⲁϣ-⟩ ⟦relat⟧{annotation: relative} (⟨ⲉⲧⲁϩ-⟩ ⟪A⟫)⟦;⟧ converse (⟦esp⟧{annotation: especially} ⟪Sa⟫, ⟦‹v›⟧{annotation: vide} ⟦Ep 1 244⟧{reference: Ep}), as ⟨ϩ-⟩ ⟦vb⟧{annotation: verb}, ⟨ϩⲟⲗϥ⟩, ⟨ϩⲏⲙ⟩, ⟨ϩⲱⲡ⟩ (⟨ϣⲱⲡⲓ⟩ ⟦Eccl 2 7⟧{bible: Ecclesiastes 2:7} ⟪F⟫), ⟨ⲥⲟⲉⲓϩ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲩⲁϩ⸗⟩ (⟦Job 30 14⟧{bible: Job 30:14}), ⟨ⲟⲩⲟϩⲥ†⟩ (⟦Sh ViK 9298⟧{reference: Sh Vi K}).
¶ Replaces ⟨ϫ⟩, as ⟨ϣⲉ⟩ (⟦ST 378⟧{reference: ST}), ⟨ϣⲱ⟩ (⟦He 9 20⟧{bible: Epistle to the Hebrews 9:20}), ⟨ϣⲱⲛϥ⟩ (⟦Miss 8 210⟧{reference: Miss}), ⟨ϣⲟⲩⲧⲏ⟩ (Louvre ⟦R 49⟧{reference: R}), ⟨ϣⲡⲟ⟩ (⟦PSBA 19 214⟧{reference: PSBA}), ⟨ⲙⲁⲁϣⲉ⟩ (⟦Ac 7 57⟧{bible: Acts of the Apostles 7:57}), ⟨ϣⲁϣⲉ⟩ (Louvre ⟦E⟧{annotation: East} 14251)⟦;⟧ ⟦esp⟧{annotation: especially} after ⟨ⲧ⟩, as ⟨ⲁⲧϣⲃⲓⲛ⟩ (⟦BMar 163⟧{reference: B Mar}), ⟨ⲧϣⲟⲗⲥ⟩ (⟦P 44 57⟧{reference: P})⟦;⟧ converse ⟪B⟫, as ⟨ϫⲱⲗϩ⟩ (⟦Cat 8⟧{reference: Cat}), ⟨ⲥⲁⲛⲟⲩϫϥ⟩ (⟦Va 75 54⟧{reference: Va}), ⟨ϫⲱⲕ⟩ (⟦2 Cor 11 25⟧{bible: Second Epistle to the Corinthians 11:25}), ⟨ϩⲟⲣϫ⟩ (⟦‹ib› 10 10⟧{bible: Second Epistle to the Corinthians 10:10}) & after ⟨ⲧ⟩, as ⟨ⲉⲧϫⲏⲕ⟩ (⟦Job 12 22⟧{bible: Job 12:22}), ⟨ⲉⲧϫⲓⲕⲉⲓ⟩ (⟪S⟫ ⟨ϣⲓⲕⲉ⟩ ⟦Eccl 10 8⟧{bible: Ecclesiastes 10:8} ⟪F⟫), ⟨ⲙⲉⲧϫⲙⲏⲛⲉ⟩ (⟦Miss 4 763⟧{reference: Miss}) ⟨ⲙⲉⲧϫⲁⲛⲁϩⲧⲩⲃ⟩ (⟦BM 517⟧{reference: BM}), ⟨ⲧϫⲓ⟩ ⟨gloss: daughter⟩ (⟦Stegemann 67⟧{reference: Stegemann}). For ⟨ⲧϣ⟩ = ⟨ϫ⟩ ⟦‹v›⟧{annotation: vide} ⟨ϫ⟩.
¶ Replaces ⟨ϭ⟩ ⟪S⟫, as ⟨ⲛⲟⲩϣⲥ⟩ (⟦Deu 28 22⟧{bible: Deuteronomy 28:22}), ⟨ⲛⲟϣ⟩ (⟦Mor 38 57⟧{reference: Mor}), ⟨ⲥⲏϣ⟩ (⟦RNC 54⟧{reference: RNC})⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ϭⲓⲛⲉ⟩ corrected to ⟨ϣ.⟩ (⟦PS 251⟧{reference: PS})⟦;⟧ often ⟪B⟫, as ⟨ϣⲓ⟩ (⟦C 86 333⟧{reference: C}), ⟨ϣⲁⲗⲉ⟩ (⟦C 43 79⟧{reference: C}), ⟨ⲉϣⲟⲩ⟩ ⟨gloss: tongs⟩ (⟦C 86 271⟧{reference: C}), ⟨ⲙⲟⲣϣⲛⲁϩ⟩ (⟦P 54 170⟧{reference: P})⟦;⟧ converse, as ⟨ϭⲗⲟϥ⟩ (⟦TEuch 2 279⟧{reference: T Euch}), ⟨ϭⲱⲣⲡ⟩ (⟦1 Cor 14 6⟧{bible: First Epistle to the Corinthians 14:6}), ⟨ϭⲁⲧⲥ⟩ (⟦Ez 39 17⟧{bible: Ezekiel 39:17}), ⟨ϭⲗⲏⲓⲙⲓ⟩ (⟦K 194⟧{reference: K})⟦;⟧ ⟦‹cf›⟧{annotation: confer} ⟨ϣϭⲛⲏⲛ⟩, ⟨ϭϣⲛ.⟩ (⟦Pro 10 12⟧{bible: Proverbs 10:12}, ⟦Mt 12 19⟧{bible: Gospel of St. Matthew 12:19}), ⟨ϭⲛ.⟩
¶ Transcribed in ⟦Gk⟧{annotation: Greek} by ⟨σζ⟩, as ⟨σζορπ⟩ (⟦MR 5 41⟧{reference: MR}), ⟨σζτωρι⟩ (⟦‹ib› 2 57⟧{reference: MR}), ⟨σζωπι⟩ (⟦CMSS 59⟧{reference: CMSS}), ⟨μασζτζ⟩ (⟦EW 86⟧{reference: EW})⟦;⟧ ⟦‹cf›⟧{annotation: confer} names ⟨Ρασζιδ⟩, ⟨Σζεριχ⟩ (⟦PLond 4⟧{reference: P Lond}), ⟨Ισζεμ⟩ (⟦Ryl 346⟧{reference: Ryl})⟦;⟧ otherwise = ⟨σ⟩, as ⟨Σενούθιος⟩, ⟨Ψενπνούθης⟩, ⟨Σενῆσις⟩, ⟨Πουῶνσις⟩, ⟨Ψόις⟩, ⟨Τσελέετ⟩, ⟨Τσέερε⟩.

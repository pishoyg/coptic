629a

¶ ⟨ϧ⟩, 28th letter of alphabet, called ⟨ϧⲉⲓ⟩ (⟦K 1⟧{reference: K}), ⟨ϧⲁⲓ⟩ ⟨خاي⟩ (⟦Stern p 418⟧{reference: Stern}). Only in ⟪B⟫⟪O⟫, ⟦om⟧{annotation: omits, omitted} in ⟪S⟫ alphabets (⟦Hall pll 28, 29⟧{reference: Hall}, ‹Class Philol› 16 193)⟦;⟧ corresponding regularly to ⟪S⟫⟪A2⟫⟪F⟫ ⟨ϩ⟩, ⟪A⟫ ⟨ⳉ⟩. Derived from ⟦hierogl⟧{annotation: hieroglyphic} ⟨ḫ⟩, as ⟨ϧⲏⲃⲥ⟩, ⟨ϧⲣⲱⲟⲩ⟩, ⟨ⲁϧⲱⲙ⟩, ⟨ⲱⲥϧ⟩, or ⟨ẖ⟩, as ⟨ϧⲙⲟⲙ⟩, ⟨ϧⲱⲧⲉⲃ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲁϧⲧ⟩, ⟨ⲫⲁϧⲣⲓ⟩. Found in ⟪O⟫ (⟦Hor⟧{reference: Hor}) as ⟨ϧ⟩ (made always thus in 2 strokes) in ⟨ϧⲛ-⟩, ⟨ϧⲣⲱⲧ⟩, ⟨ⲱⲛϧ⟩ ⟦&c⟧{annotation: et cetera}, in ⟦PMich 6131⟧{reference: P Mich} as ⟨ϧ⟩: ⟨ϧⲉⲗ⟩. In ⟦DM⟧{reference: DM} glosses thrice: ⟨ⲁϧ⟩, ⟨ϧⲱ⟩, ⟨ϧϫⲉⲓ⟩. Sporadically in non-literary ⟪F⟫⟪Bf⟫, as ⟦CMSS 25, 52, 57⟧{reference: CMSS}, ⟦BM 590 (2), 626⟧{reference: BM} (⟦‹cf›⟧{annotation: confer} ‹Amer JPhilol› 56 105). Rarely replaced by ⟨ⲭ⟩, as ⟦BM 1237⟧{reference: BM}, ⟦Ryl 462⟧{reference: Ryl}, except where ⟦Gk⟧{annotation: Greek} alphabet employed, as ⟦MR 5 41⟧{reference: MR}, ⟦CMSS 59⟧{reference: CMSS}. In late ⟪S⟫ improperly, as ⟦PChass 1⟧{reference: P Chass} ⟨ⲡⲁϧⲣⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲧⲱⲣϧ⟩, ⟨ⲧⲱϧ⟩ (error ⟦?⟧{annotation: perhaps, possibly} for ⟨ϩ⟩)⟦;⟧ in this & ⟦Bodl (P) a 1⟧{reference: Bodl (P) a} also as ⟨ϩ́⟩: ⟨ⲡⲁϩ́ⲣⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲁϩ́⟩, ⟨ϩ́ⲏⲧϥ⟩ (not so in duplicate ⟦(P) a 3⟧{reference: Bodl (P) a}), ⟦PMéd⟧{reference: P Méd} ⟨ⲗⲟϩ́ⲙ⟩, ⟨ϩ́ⲱϩ́⟩. Transcribes ⟦Ar⟧{annotation: Arabic} ⟨خ⟩, as ⟨ⲭⲁⲗϧ⟩ ⟨كلخ⟩ (⟦P 43 33⟧{reference: P}), ⟨ⲁϧⲁⲣ⟩ ⟨اخر⟩, ⟨ⲓⲉϧⲟⲇ⟩ ⟨يأخذ⟩, ⟨ϣⲉⲓϧ⟩ ⟨شيخ⟩ (⟦EW 234 ff⟧{reference: EW})⟦;⟧ rarely ⟨ح⟩, as ⟨ϧⲗⲟⲩⲁ⟩ ⟨حلوة⟩ (⟦PChass 2⟧{reference: P Chass})⟦;⟧ transcribed by ⟨خ⟩, as ⟨خان⟩ ⟨ϧⲉⲛ-⟩, ⟨خات⟩ ⟨ϧⲏⲧ⟩ (⟦BIF 5 106⟧{reference: BIF}).

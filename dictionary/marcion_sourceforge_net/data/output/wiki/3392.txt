¶ ⟨ⲏ⟩, seventh letter of alphabet, called ⟨ϩⲏⲧⲁ⟩ ⟪S⟫ (⟦My 64⟧{reference: My}), ⟨ⲏⲧⲁ⟩ (⟦Leyd 434⟧{reference: Leyd}), ⟨ⲏⲓⲧⲁ⟩ (⟦K 1⟧{reference: K}), ⟨ϩⲁⲧⲉ⟩ ⟪B⟫ (⟦Stern p 418⟧{reference: Stern}), ⟨هادة⟩, ⟨هيدة⟩ (latter especially ⟪B⟫ according to ⟦P 43 239⟧{reference: P}). As numeral ⟦m⟧{annotation: masculine} & ⟦f⟧{annotation: feminine} = ⟨gloss: eight⟩ ⟪B⟫⟪F⟫ (⟦Ac 9 33⟧{bible: Acts of the Apostles 9:33}), rarely ⟪S⟫ (⟦Br 132⟧{reference: Br}). Represents vowels unwritten in earlier ⟦hierogl⟧{annotation: hieroglyphic}, though visible in certain half-consonants in later periods.
¶ ⟨bullet: a.⟩ dialectal interchange is irregular: final ⟪S⟫ ⟨ⲉ⟩ often = ⟪F⟫ ⟨ⲏ⟩, otherwise ⟨ⲏ⟩ for ⟨ⲉ⟩ mostly mere inaccuracy, often in ⟪F⟫ documents, as ⟦Kr 228⟧{reference: Kr}.
¶ ⟨bullet: b.⟩ in ⟦Gk⟧{annotation: Greek} words ⟨ⲏ⟩ for ⟨ⲉ⟩ rarely, as ⟨ⲁⲏⲧⲟⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲉⲛⲧⲏⲗⲉⲩⲏ⟩ (⟦J 92 21⟧{reference: J}), ⟨ⲧⲣⲏⲫⲁ⟩ (⟨τρέφειν⟩ ⟦DeV 56⟧{reference: De V})⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲁⲓⲥⲑⲉⲥⲓⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲉⲥⲩⲭⲓⲁ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲕⲉⲩⲉ⟩.
¶ ⟨bullet: c.⟩ for ⟨ⲓ⟩, as ⟨ⲁⲕⲣⲏⲃⲓⲁ⟩, ⟨ⲕⲣⲏⲧⲏⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲡⲟⲗⲏⲧⲓⲁ⟩⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲁⲑⲗⲓⲧⲏⲥ⟩, ⟨ⲉⲧⲓⲙⲁ⟩ (⟨αἴτημα⟩), ⟨ⲡⲓⲅⲏ⟩.
¶ ⟨bullet: d.⟩ for ⟨ⲩ⟩, as ⟨ⲉⲛⲧⲏⲙⲁ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲏⲥⲧⲏⲣⲓⲟⲛ⟩⟦;⟧ converse (oftenest) ⟨ⲁⲡⲟⲑⲩⲕⲏ⟩, ⟨ⲕⲩⲣⲓⲝ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲩⲛⲉⲩⲉ⟩ (⟦PS 222⟧{reference: PS}), ⟨ⲥⲡⲩⲗⲁⲓⲟⲛ⟩, ⟨ⲯⲏⲭⲩ⟩ (⟦R 1 2 44⟧{reference: R}).
¶ ⟨bullet: e.⟩ for ⟨ⲟⲓ⟩, as ⟨ⲉⲡⲏⲅⲓⲟⲛ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲧⲏⲭⲉⲓ⟩⟦;⟧ converse ⟨ⲕⲁⲧⲟⲓⲅⲱⲣⲉⲓ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲓⲣⲡ⟩ (⟦BM 1063⟧{reference: BM}).
¶ Esne-Edfû ⟦MSS⟧{annotation: manuscripts} ⟪S⟫ often double ⟨ⲏ⟩, as ⟨ⲏⲏⲡ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲏⲏⲧⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲩⲏⲏϩ⟩, ⟨ϭⲣⲏⲏⲡⲉ⟩ (all in ⟦BAp⟧{reference: B Ap}).

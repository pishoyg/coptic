¶ ⟨ⲣ⟩, 17th letter of alphabet, called ⟨ⲣⲱ⟩ (⟦My 34, 90⟧{reference: My}), ⟨ϩⲣⲟ⟩ (⟦P 131^(4) 77⟧{reference: P}) ⟪S⟫, ⟨ⲣⲟ⟩ ⟪B⟫ (⟦Stern p 418⟧{reference: Stern}, ⟦K 1⟧{reference: K}), ⟨روو⟩ (⟦Stern ‹l c›⟧{reference: Stern}), ⟨روا⟩ (⟦MG 17 368⟧{reference: MG}), ⟨اهرا⟩ (⟦P 43 247⟧{reference: P}). As numeral ⟪B⟫⟪F⟫, rarely ⟪S⟫ = ⟨gloss: hundred⟩ (⟨ϣⲉ⟩). Derived from ⟦hierogl⟧{annotation: hieroglyphic} ⟨r⟩, as ⟨ⲣⲱⲙⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲣⲡⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲡⲉⲓⲣⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲙⲟⲩⲣ⟩⟦;⟧ rarely from ⟨n⟩, as ⟨ⲣⲙϩⲉ⟩ ⟦(?)⟧{annotation: perhaps, possibly}, ⟨ϩⲉⲣⲙⲁⲛ⟩, ⟨ⲡⲁⲣⲙϩⲟⲧⲡ⟩ (⟦RChamp 721⟧{reference: R Champ}). Often lost, as ⟨ϣ⟩, ⟨ⲉϣ⟩ ⟦vb⟧{annotation: verb}, ⟨ⲙⲉ⟩ ⟨gloss: love⟩, ⟨ϩⲟⲟⲩ⟩ ⟨gloss: day⟩, ⟨ⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲥⲁϩⲟⲩ⟩ (⟦Sethe ‹Verbum› 1 235 ff⟧{reference: Sethe Verbum}), less often palatalized, as ⟨ⲕⲁⲓⲥⲉ⟩, ⟨ⲟⲩⲟⲉⲓϣ⟩, ⟨ϩⲓⲏ⟩, ⟨ϩⲕⲟⲉⲓⲧ⟩ (⟦‹l c›⟧{annotation: loco citato} 243, ⟦AZ 27 106⟧{reference: AZ}). Varies with ⟨ⲗ⟩, ⟦‹q v›⟧{annotation: quod vide} ⟨bullet: a⟩ ⟨bullet: b⟩ & ⟦Ep 1 243⟧{reference: Ep}. ⟨ⲛ⟩ before ⟨ⲣ⟩ assimilated ⟪S⟫ (archaic), as ⟨ⲣⲣⲟⲙⲡⲉ⟩ (⟦Ap 20 5⟧{bible: Apocalypse 20:5}), ⟨ⲣⲣⲟⲟⲩϣ⟩ (⟦PS 219⟧{reference: PS}), ⟨ⲣⲣⲁⲥⲟⲩ⟩ (⟦Sa 18 19⟧{bible: Wisdom of Solomon 18:19}) & ⟪A⟫, as ⟨ⲣⲣⲙⲛⲕⲏⲙⲉ⟩ (⟦Ex 1 15⟧{bible: Exodus 1:15}), ⟨ⲣⲣⲟ⟩ (⟦Pro 14 19⟧{bible: Proverbs 14:19}), but for ⟨ⲗⲗ⟩ sometimes ⟨ⲣⲗ⟩, as ⟨ⲫⲟⲣⲗⲟⲥ⟩ (⟦PLond 4 464⟧{reference: P Lond}), ⟨ⲥⲓⲕⲉⲣⲗⲛ⟩ (⟦‹ib› 523⟧{reference: P Lond}), ⟨ⲡⲣⲗⲁϯⲟⲛ⟩ (⟦JKP 2 54⟧{reference: JKP}), ⟨ⲃⲣⲗⲉ⟩ (⟦Faras 3 pl 68 15⟧{reference: Faras}). Intrusive in ⟨ⲙⲁⲣⲕⲉⲇⲟⲛⲓⲟⲥ⟩ (⟦BMis 443⟧{reference: B Mis}). Transcribes ⟦Ar⟧{annotation: Arabic} ⟨ر⟩, as ⟨ⲁⲭⲡⲁⲣ⟩ ⟨اكبر⟩, ⟨ⲉⲣⲣⲁⲡ⟩ ⟨الرب⟩, ⟨ⲣⲁϩⲙⲉⲑ⟩ ⟨حمة⟩ (⟦EW 234 ff⟧{reference: EW}), ⟨ⲕⲁⲣⲟⲟⲣⲉ⟩ ⟨قارورة⟩, ⟨ϩⲁϭⲁⲣ⟩ ⟨حجر⟩ (⟦AZ 23 117, 118⟧{reference: AZ})⟦;⟧ transcribed by ⟨ر⟩, as ⟨ارمنت⟩ ⟨ⲉⲣⲙⲟⲛⲧ⟩, ⟨امشير⟩ ⟨ⲙϣⲓⲣ⟩, ⟨هدرا⟩ ⟨ϩⲁⲧⲣⲉ⟩, ⟨برنوج⟩ ⟨ⲡⲉⲣⲛⲟⲩϫ⟩.

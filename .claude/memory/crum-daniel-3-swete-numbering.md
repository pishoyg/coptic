Crum's Daniel 3 24–90 citations follow Swete, *The Old Testament in Greek*
vol. 3, Theodotion text (Codex B). The OCR text is at
archive.org/download/theoldtestamenti03swetuoft/theoldtestamenti03swetuoft_djvu.txt
(Daniel 3 is around lines 48400–49000).

- Swete prints the Song in the order 53, 54 (depths), 55 (throne), …, 57, 59,
  58, 60, …, 66, 71, 72, 69, 70, 73. The SSACS Coptic (foreign chapter B) has
  the same order. SSACS's Greek column follows Rahlfs's numerical order, so it
  is out of step with the Coptic and is no guide.
- Swete's verse 40 begins at "ὡς ἐν ὁλοκαυτώσει κριῶν" (the end of Rahlfs's
  39), so Swete's 40 straddles SSACS B 16 and B 17.
- Swete's 31–33 division matches Rahlfs (in both the Θ' and the OG columns).

**How to apply:** when a Dan 3 citation looks off, check Swete before calling
it Crum's error. The mapping lives in the `VERSE.daniel` table in
`docs/crum/septuagint.ts`. Related: [[wiki-tsv-is-sheet-snapshot]].

The Compléments scans print the Crum page and column beside entries (`237 a`
beside `ⲛⲟⲩϣⲥ`), and in the running head (`[235 b ⲛⲟⲩⲟⲩϩ]`). An unmarked entry
falls under the last marker above it.

**How to apply:** when auditing the `Marcion` column of `complements.tsv`, check
that the key's Crum page matches the margin. This separates homonyms and
near-homographs that a headword match can't. A marginal page doesn't prove that
Crum has the word: Kasser also slots new words in by alphabetical position.
